# 37 — Policy architecture

## What this is

An architecture diagram, a data-classification table, an access policy, and backup/DR numbers: daily encrypted backups, RPO 24h, RTO 8h, quarterly restore test.

## Why it matters

A policy is the sentence you use when two people disagree. The numbers make the sentence checkable.

## How to run

Read `POLICY.md` and open `architecture.mmd`.

## Portfolio deliverable

The four classification labels rewritten for a clinic or a small shop you invent.

# 16 — Vulnerability lab report

## What this is

A report template and one sample finding: reflected XSS on a lab app such as Juice Shop or DVWA. There is no exploit code.

## Why it matters

A finding without evidence and a fix is a rumor. The template forces those fields.

## How to run

Read `SAMPLE_FINDING.md`, then copy `REPORT_TEMPLATE.md` and fill it after a legal lab exercise.

## Portfolio deliverable

One completed finding in your own words, with the lab name and the date, and no copied walkthrough.

## Exercise

Copy REPORT_TEMPLATE.md after a Juice Shop or DVWA session. Fill evidence and remediation. Do not paste a public walkthrough.

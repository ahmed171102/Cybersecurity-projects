# Incident playbook

1. **Prepare** — you already have logs, a contact, and a decision maker.
2. **Detect** — write the facts you can prove. Time, host, account.
3. **Contain** — stop it spreading. Disable the account or isolate the host. Do not wipe the disk first.
4. **Eradicate** — remove the extra account and the exposed password.
5. **Recover** — bring the service back from a backup you have tested, then watch it.
6. **Learn** — change one control: MFA, an alert, or a firewall rule.
